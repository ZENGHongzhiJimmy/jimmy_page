About

Dedicated educator, innovator, and researcher

Dr LUI Wing Cheung Richard is currently a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Computing at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). He earned his Bachelor’s, Master’s, and PhD degrees in Computer Science from the University of Hong Kong.

He has been recognized for his teaching excellence with multiple PolyU teaching awards, including the Faculty Teaching Award for Individual Outstanding Performance and several team awards. He is also deeply engaged in educational innovation and has led UGC-funded initiatives in innovative learning approaches, gamified learning, cloud virtual laboratories, and Generative AI-powered tutoring systems.

His projects have reached international recognition, including a finalist placement in QS Reimagine Education 2024 and a Silver Award in the “AI in Education” category in 2025.
